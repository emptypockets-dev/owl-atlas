THE OWL ATLAS · TYPOGRAPHY REVIEW

Three display serifs on the real screens.

The same five specimens — hero, the 404 set piece, a chapter opening on paper, figures and Greek, and small headings — drawn four times over: the Georgia we ship today, and three OFL candidates. Colours, sizes, letter-spacing and line-height are taken from src/styles.css, so what changes between settings is the face and nothing else.

Switch faces with the radios above, or press Compare side by side to see all four at once. Fraunces is variable: its opsz, SOFT and WONK sliders appear whenever Fraunces is on screen. The disc in the hero is a plain gradient placeholder, not a coin photograph.

Any setting can be linked to: ?face=didot, ?compare=1, ?only=d for one specimen on its own, or a #hero / #year404 / #chapter / #greek / #smalls anchor. They combine — ?compare=1&only=d puts the four Greek settings side by side.
