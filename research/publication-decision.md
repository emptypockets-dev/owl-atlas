# Public launch decision — 17 September 2026

The owner selected **theowlatlas.com**, purchased separately at Squarespace,
as the public address for the existing Owl Atlas research edition on the
personal Vercel project in `emptypocketsdevs-projects`.

When asked how the six BnF/Gallica photographs with unresolved reuse conditions
should appear at public launch, the owner explicitly chose:

> Keep the six photos with their existing credits and reuse-review notices.

This records a publication choice, **not rights clearance**. All six
`reuseStatus: "review-pending"` flags, individual credits, original source links,
policy links and visible caveats remain. No new permission has been obtained,
and the implementation's MIT license does not cover the photographs.
The standard remote-image build is used; no image archiving was authorized
or performed as part of connecting the domain.

The apex domain is canonical; `www` redirects to it. Public production is
intended to be discoverable by search engines. Generated deployment URLs and
previews retain Vercel Standard Protection. Deployments remain manual so a push
alone does not publish new editorial material.
