THE SAME DESIGN IS NOT THE SAME COIN

Start with the object.
Then the number.

A well-centered Athena, a complete helmet crest, an undisturbed surface: details that help us read an owl also shape its appeal to collectors. A wear grade captures only part of that picture. 3636Numismatic Guaranty Company

For a useful comparison, begin with the same coin family and denomination. Then compare the strike, surfaces and both faces. Keep archaic, classical, Pi-style and New Style results separate.

03 / READ THE WHOLE PRICE

The winning bid
is only the beginning.

The hammer is the winning bid. The buyer premium is the auction house's added fee. Tax and shipping come afterward; the seller's proceeds depend on a separate agreement.

How this snapshot was assembled—and what it cannot tell us

Current Heritage comparisons use two specified consecutive lot ranges, including the coins with listed problems. Historical comparisons are a small, non-random selection of the same reported grade and strike/surface scores at the same auction house. Raw CNG grades are not assumed equivalent to NGC grades. No worldwide average, annual return or price forecast is implied.

Public archive pages were checked on 17 September 2026; some were cached. One August Heritage archive marks a lot sold at $1,442.04 while a cached individual page still shows an earlier live bid. The ledger preserves that conflict and uses the archive result. Several incomplete or conflicting leads were excluded.

No invoice settlement, returns, undisclosed restoration or authenticity was independently verified. Seller net proceeds are unknown. A sell-through rate would also require unsold listings, which this sold-only dataset does not contain.

For a stronger eBay study, actual accepted-offer data should be grouped by type, condition, selling format and period; repeat listings should be deduplicated. Replicas, jewelry, modern bullion and mixed lots need separate treatment. No auction photographs are reproduced here.

This is a dated research snapshot, not a live price feed or an appraisal. The chronological and museum-object dates elsewhere in the atlas remain distinct from auction catalogue descriptions.
